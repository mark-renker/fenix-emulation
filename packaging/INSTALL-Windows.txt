Fenix I - Windows install

1. Copy the "Fenix I.vst3" and "Fenix I FX.vst3" folders to
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
2. Copy "Fenix I.clap" and "Fenix I FX.clap" to
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\   (for Bitwig, Reaper and other CLAP hosts)
3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW. Both appear under "Renky Denk".
4. "Fenix I.exe" is the standalone app; it runs from anywhere. If Windows
   SmartScreen warns about an unrecognized app, click More info > Run anyway
   (the app is not code-signed).

Nothing sounds until it is patched: open the PATCHES menu and pick one, then
play MIDI. To retune the circuit model, copy topology_params.json to
%APPDATA%\FenixI\topology_params.json and press RELOAD PARAMS.
