Fenix I @VERSION@

An unofficial circuit-level software emulation of the Synton Fénix (I) semi-modular synthesizer: three VCOs, three LFOs, two state-variable filters and the ladder filter, three envelopes, four VCAs, mixers, ring modulators, noise, sample & hold, comparator, slew, portamento and audio-in processors, all freely patchable with banana cables.

On the next screens you can choose which formats to install for the instrument (Audio Unit, VST3, CLAP, standalone app) and for the effect version. Plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins; the app goes to /Applications.

Requires macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
