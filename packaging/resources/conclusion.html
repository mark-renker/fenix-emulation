Installed

Restart your DAW (or rescan plug-ins) and look for Renky Denk in the instrument list: Fenix I (instrument) and Fenix I FX (effect). Logic Pro and GarageBand validate Audio Units on their first launch after an install.

Nothing sounds until it is patched: open the PATCHES menu and pick one, then play MIDI. To retune the circuit model, copy topology_params.json (in the bundles zip or the repository) to ~/Library/Application Support/FenixI/ and press RELOAD PARAMS.

To uninstall, delete the plug-ins from /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components, /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP (and the app from /Applications).
